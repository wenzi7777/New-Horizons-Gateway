# studio-v2.1.2

**Adds the TIA-CTL v2.0.F board.**

- Studio, Pocket and the headless relay now recognise TIA-CTL v2.0.F: its 14 × 14 sensor matrix, external LEDs and OLED show up, its packets are sized correctly, and it updates from its own firmware channel.
- The App SDK project lists v2.0.F as a board.
