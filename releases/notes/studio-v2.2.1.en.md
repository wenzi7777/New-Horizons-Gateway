# studio-v2.2.1

**NH Studio and NH Pocket now tell you when a new version is out, while they are running.**

- Studio and Pocket used to look for updates only when they started, and showed the result in Settings. A Studio left open, or a Pocket kept alive by its relay service, never heard of a release.
- They now check again every few hours (Pocket also when it comes back to the foreground) and open an update dialog with the release notes, "Later" and the install button. "Later" applies to that version only.
- Studio's "Check for updates at start-up" switch also controls these periodic checks.

Versions before 2.2.1 do not have this: update them once from Settings.
